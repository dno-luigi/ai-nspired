Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

Our Core Privacy Promise: We believe that access to justice requires total data integrity. We will never sell, lease, trade, or distribute your personal information, web traffic patterns, or uploaded document data to any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or commercial tracking pools.

1. The Free Audit Data Exchange (EITL Framework)

To maintain an uncompromised, objective diagnostic platform, the Truth Engine core audit is provided to the public completely free of charge. In exchange for this free utility, you explicitly agree to the following data workflow:

  • Automated Processing: When you upload a document or docket, our systems run programmatic validation checks to generate an initial confidence score.
  • Expert Backend Routing: By default, your sanitized query structures may be piped to our proprietary Expert in the Loop (EITL) Workspace Backend GUI, where independent legal experts review and refine the diagnostic logic.
  • The Academy Exception: To build an open public knowledge base, fully sanitized datasets may be adapted into open-access case studies and learning modules inside The Official AI University.

2. Absolute Sanitization Guarantee

Under no circumstances are personal identifiers, client names, active case numbers, or sensitive corporate credentials transmitted or stored in our public learning systems. Our architecture executes a mandatory, destructive sanitization protocol to completely strip all personally identifiable information (PII) before any data leaves the immediate tool environment.

3. User Control & Data Opt-Out

If you are a corporate entity, practicing attorney, or pro per litigant requiring localized, zero-retention analysis, you may explicitly opt out of the data framework via your dashboard control settings prior to initiating an audit submission. Opting out ensures your data is never routed to the expert workspace backend or the academy ecosystem.

4. Cookies & Site Analytics

We use baseline infrastructure tracking (such as Cloudflare) to monitor system performance, server bandwidth, and global traffic security trends. Third-party advertising partners may place performance cookies strictly to serve context-appropriate tech, legal SaaS, and utility ads on our dashboard layouts.
